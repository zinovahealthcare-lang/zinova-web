Quality Policy & Regulatory Assurance

Zinova Healthcare is committed to uncompromised safety and efficacy. Every pharmaceutical formulation adheres strictly to ISO standards and cGMP guidelines through rigorous testing protocols.

Our Quality Standards

Built upon strict global regulatory compliance and advanced laboratory controls.

cGMP Compliance

Adherence to Current Good Manufacturing Practices to ensure safety, efficacy, and consistency in every batch produced.

ISO Certification

Quality management systems operational under standardized international health and pharmaceutical benchmarks.

Advanced QC Testing

Analytical testing of raw materials, active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), and packaging materials.

Batch Release Protocol

Multi-tier documentation and verification prior to the final release of any commercial formulation.

Quality Control Workflow

End-to-end oversight throughout our production lifecycle.

01

Raw Material Audit

Verification and identity testing of incoming active and inactive ingredients.

02

In-Process Inspection

Real-time continuous monitoring during mixing, granulation, and compression stages.

03

Finished Product Testing

Dissolution, assay, and microbiological evaluation against target specifications.

04

Release & Traceability

Final documentation review, batch record signing, and controlled distribution tracking.
